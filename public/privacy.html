Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short: Jaisalmer Visit (jaisalmervisit.com) is a free guide to Jaisalmer. You can use it without an account. We show Google ads to keep it free. Google Analytics cookies are used only if you click Accept in the cookie notice. We never sell your personal data.

Who we are

This website is run by Jaisalmer Visit, Jaisalmer, Rajasthan, India. Questions about this policy or your data: jaisalmervisitin@gmail.com.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • By default, our site asks Google to show non-personalised ads and not to store advertising cookies. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may also see Google's own consent message.
  • Some ads and sponsored listings come straight from local businesses. These are marked "Ad" or "Sponsored" and use no tracking cookies.

Google Analytics

If you click Accept in the cookie notice, we load Google Analytics. It tells us which pages and features are used (for example, which places are opened) so we can improve the guide. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser type and pages visited. If you click Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time: (also in the footer of every page).

What you send us

  • Add Your Service: your name, service type, mobile number and description. We use them to review and list your service. Once approved, your name and number are shown publicly so visitors can contact you.
  • Ratings of service providers: the star rating, and the optional comment and name. They are checked before they appear on the site.
  • Newsletter: your email address, used only to send Jaisalmer Visit updates. Ask us any time to remove it.

This information is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and is seen only by the site's administrators.

Your location

If you allow it, your browser shares your location so the map can show where you are and draw directions. This stays on your device: we do not send or store your location.

Stored on your device

The site saves a few settings in your browser (local storage): your cookie choice, audio guide language and speed, and your last Add Your Service draft. They never leave your device.

Links to other sites

Buttons such as WhatsApp, Call, Google Maps and YouTube open other services. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Children

This site is meant for travellers and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to jaisalmervisitin@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
