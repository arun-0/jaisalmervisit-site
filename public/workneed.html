Our story

Built with a Local Vision

Jaisalmer has no shortage of skilled hands — what's missing is an easy way to find them. Often you have to ask someone you know for a good electrician's, plumber's or mason's number, and many skilled people are never known beyond their own neighbourhood.

WorkNeed App is an effort to close that gap: a simple app where local work and skilled services can be found digitally, and where workers can make their skills their identity.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WorkNeed App?

WorkNeed App is an Android and iPhone app for finding local jobs and skilled workers. Customers can search workers by category and location and view their profiles with contact details; workers can add their own skills and services.

Who can use WorkNeed App?

Anyone who needs a local worker for a job, and any skilled worker who wants to list their services and find work opportunities nearby.

Which types of skilled workers are on WorkNeed App?

The app is organised by work category, including electricians, plumbers, carpenters, cleaners, drivers, building masons and other skilled trades. Availability in each category depends on who has listed in your area.

How can I download WorkNeed App?

WorkNeed App is available on the Google Play Store for Android phones and on the App Store for iPhone. Tap the Google Play or App Store button on this page (Android users can also scan the QR code with their phone), then install it from the store listing.

How can workers join WorkNeed App?

Install the app from Google Play, then add your skills and services so customers searching your category and location can find your profile and contact you.
