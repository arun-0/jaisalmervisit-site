## What to expect

- Embroidery and mirror-work
- Patchwork and textiles
- Leather goods
- Local craft shops

## Where and when

Old-city lanes, Gandhi Chowk and fort-area shops.

Late afternoon, when it is cooler.

## Before you go

Ask about the maker, material and washing instructions. Look at the reverse of embroidered pieces and check that the colours and seams suit the way you plan to use them.
