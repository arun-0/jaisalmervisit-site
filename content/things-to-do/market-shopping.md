## What to expect

- Lively bazaar lanes
- Souvenirs and gifts
- Browsing and bargaining

## Where and when

Gandhi Chowk and old-city bazaars.

Late afternoon, when it is cooler.

## Before you go

Allow time to look at more than one shop. For delicate purchases, ask how the piece was made and how to pack it for your journey home.
