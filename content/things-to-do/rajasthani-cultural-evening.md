## What to expect

- Live folk music
- Traditional dance, including Kalbeliya
- Traditional costume and instruments
- An evening out under the stars

## Where and when

Desert camps and city venues.

After sunset.

An evening.

## Before you go

Ask which musicians or dancers are on that evening, when the performance begins and whether a meal or transport is part of the ticket.
