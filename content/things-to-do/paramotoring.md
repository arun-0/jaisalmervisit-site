## What to expect

- Bird's-eye view of the dunes
- Motorised paraglider flight
- Aerial photos and video

## Where and when

Sam dunes.

Morning or late afternoon, in calm weather.

## Before you go

Confirm the pilot’s experience, the flight time in the air and the weather policy before paying. Make clear whether photographs or a recording are included.
