## What to expect

- Dark desert skies
- Stars and constellations
- Night photography
- Often paired with a desert camp

## Where and when

Sam / Khuri.

Clear, moonless nights.

## Before you go

Check the moon phase and cloud cover for your date. Ask your host how far the viewing spot is from the camp lights and how you will get back in the dark.
