## What to expect

- A short flight over the dunes
- Wide desert views from the air
- Photos and video from above

## Where and when

Sam dunes.

Morning or late afternoon, in calm weather.

## Before you go

Ask the operator to explain the launch and landing, equipment checks and the cancellation policy if the wind changes. Confirm any passenger restrictions directly.
