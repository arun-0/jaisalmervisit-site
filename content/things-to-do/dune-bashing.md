## What to expect

- 4x4 drive over sand dunes
- Steep dune climbs and drops
- Group fun

## Where and when

Sam dunes.

Late afternoon.

## Before you go

Ask which vehicle and seats are offered, whether everyone has a working seat belt and whether a gentler route is available. Agree the pickup point before leaving camp.
