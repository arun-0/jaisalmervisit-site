## What to expect

- Jeep ride over desert tracks
- Open desert landscapes
- Photo stops
- Sunset from the sand

## Where and when

Sam / Khuri dunes.

Late afternoon into sunset.

## Before you go

Agree the route, the number of passengers and where the jeep will stop for photos. A transfer to the dunes and a drive on desert tracks are different bookings.
