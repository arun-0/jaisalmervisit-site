## Compare the outing, not just the price

[View jeep safari details and local options](/#things-to-do?activity=jeep-safari). Ask the provider to confirm your dates, group size and route. Directory availability and a displayed price do not confirm a reservation.

A city transfer, a drive on desert tracks and dune bashing are different outings. Ask which one you are being quoted for and whether the full group has a private vehicle or shares with others.

## What to expect

- Jeep ride over desert tracks
- Open desert landscapes
- Photo stops
- Sunset from the sand

## Where and when

Sam / Khuri dunes.

Late afternoon into sunset.

## Questions to ask before booking

- Where does the outing start, and are city pickup and return included?
- How long is the desert drive, excluding transfers and waiting?
- Which stops, meals or camp activities are included?
- What is the total for your group and the cancellation policy?

Confirm seating and seat belts for every passenger. Discuss whether the route and driving style suit everyone in your group before paying.

## Before you go

Agree the route, the number of passengers and where the jeep will stop for photos. A transfer to the dunes and a drive on desert tracks are different bookings.
