## What to expect

- Golden light on the dunes
- Photography
- Easy to pair with a camel or jeep ride

## Where and when

Sam / Khuri.

An hour or so before sunset.

## Before you go

Choose a meeting point you can find after dusk. Ask your driver how much walking the stop involves and agree when to start the return journey.
