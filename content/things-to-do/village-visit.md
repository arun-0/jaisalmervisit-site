## What to expect

- Traditional village homes
- Local crafts and daily life
- Quiet desert landscape

## Where and when

Khuri and nearby villages.

Morning or late afternoon.

## Before you go

Arrange the visit with a local host. Agree on any guiding fee, ask before entering homes, and get permission before taking or sharing portraits.
