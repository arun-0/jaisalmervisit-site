## Find a safari that fits your visit

[View camel safari details and local options](/#things-to-do?activity=camel-safari). Use the directory to make an enquiry, then confirm your dates and pickup with the provider. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Choose between a short ride, a sunset outing and a camp package before comparing quotes. The total excursion time may include driving and waiting, so ask how much time is actually spent riding.

## What to expect

- Camel ride across the dunes
- Golden dunes
- Sunset views
- Photography

## Where and when

Sam / Khuri.

Best at sunset.

Typically 1–2 hrs.

## Compare quotes before paying

- Location: Sam or Khuri, the exact pickup point and the dune route.
- Timing: actual riding time, sunset stop and return time.
- Inclusions: city transfers, food, water and any camp visit.
- Total: the price for the whole group, extras and cancellation terms.

Ask about animal care and whether you can visit the dunes without riding. Share any practical access needs directly with the operator before committing.

## Before you go

Ask how long you will actually ride, which stretch is on foot and whether pickup and the return ride are included. Leave time to watch the sunset off the camel.
