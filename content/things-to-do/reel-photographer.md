## What to expect

- Short vertical videos for Reels and Shorts
- Help with poses, walks and transitions
- Fort lanes, havelis, lake and dunes as backdrops
- Edited clips, often with music

## Where and when

Fort, havelis, Gadisar Lake and the dunes.

Early morning or the hour before sunset.

## Before you go

Agree the locations, number of edited clips, delivery date and who can publish the footage. Ask before filming other visitors or people at home.
