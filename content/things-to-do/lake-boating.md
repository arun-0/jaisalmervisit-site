## What to expect

- Boat ride on the lake
- Shrines along the shore
- Soft light on the water

## Where and when

Gadisar Lake.

Early morning or around sunset.

## Before you go

At the lakeside counter, check the boat type, time on the water and return point. Ask for a suitable life jacket for each passenger before boarding.
