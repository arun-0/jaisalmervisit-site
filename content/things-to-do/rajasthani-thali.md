## What to expect

- A full meal served on one platter
- Regional Rajasthani flavours
- Dal, breads and sweets

## Where and when

Restaurants across the old city.

Lunch or dinner.

## Before you go

Ask what is on the thali that day, which items can be refilled and whether drinks and sweets are included. Tell the restaurant about dietary needs before ordering.
