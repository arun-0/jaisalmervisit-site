## Day 1 — Easy city pace

### Morning

Jaisalmer Fort, at a relaxed pace with breaks.

### Afternoon

Patwon Ki Haveli, Nathmal Ki Haveli and Salim Singh Ki Haveli, all within walking distance of each other.

### Evening

Suli Dungari for best Sunset with full Golden Fort Image and peace

## Day 2 — Desert, kept simple

### Morning

Gadisar Lake with a short boat ride.

### Afternoon

Bada Bagh and Kuldhara.

### Evening

Sam Sand Dunes — a short camel ride and sunset, back before it gets late.
