## Day 1 — Fort & lake, compact

### Morning

Jaisalmer Fort and its inner lanes, including the Jain temples.

### Afternoon

Patwon Ki Haveli, Nathmal Ki Haveli and Salim Singh Ki Haveli, all within walking distance of each other.

### Evening

Choose Gadisar Lake for a waterside walk or Suli Dungar for a view back over the fort. Pick one if sunset is close.

A dune trip needs its own half-day — skip it if you only have one day, or swap the lake evening for a rushed Sam Dunes sunset if a taxi is arranged in advance.
