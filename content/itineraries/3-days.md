## Day 1 — The city

### Morning

Jaisalmer Fort and inner lanes.

### Afternoon

Patwon Ki Haveli, Nathmal Ki Haveli and Salim Singh Ki Haveli, all within walking distance of each other.

### Evening

Suli Dungari for best Sunset with full Golden Fort Image and peace

## Day 2 — Desert day trip

### Morning

Gadisar Lake with a short boat ride.

### Afternoon

Kuldhara.

### Evening

Sam Sand Dunes for sunset, camel or jeep safari and other activites

## Day 3 — Slow morning, quiet desert

### Morning

Bada Bagh cenotaphs at the time return from Sam Dunes

### Afternoon

Army War Museum

### Evening

Khuri village for a quieter dune walk.

If you camped overnight at Sam or Khuri, use this day in reverse and return to the city by afternoon.
