## Day 1 — The city

### Morning

Jaisalmer Fort and inner lanes.

### Afternoon

Patwon Ki Haveli, Nathmal Ki Haveli and Salim Singh Ki Haveli, all within walking distance of each other.

### Evening

Suli Dungari for best Sunset with full Golden Fort reels & Peace moments

## Day 2 — The desert

### Morning

Gadisar Lake with a short boat ride.

### Afternoon

Bada Bagh cenotaphs and Kuldhara Amandon Village

### Evening

Sam Sand Dunes for sunset and a camel or jeep safari or other activities.
