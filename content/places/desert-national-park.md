## What makes it worth visiting

A rare chance to see genuine desert wildlife and landscape — sand dunes, scrub, and salt flats — rather than just the tourist-facing dune fields.

## What to look for

- Great Indian bustard and other desert birds, seasonally
- Rocky and sandy desert terrain
- Salt water lakes within the park

## When to go

Early morning for wildlife spotting, and cooler temperatures.

## Getting there

Southwest of Jaisalmer, toward Sam.

~40 minutes by taxi from the fort.

## Visitor tips

Wildlife sightings, especially the bustard, are not guaranteed — go with realistic expectations and a good guide.
