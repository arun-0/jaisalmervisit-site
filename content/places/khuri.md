## What makes it worth visiting

A calmer alternative to Sam — smaller crowds, traditional mud-and-thatch houses, and dunes right at the edge of the village.

## What to look for

- Traditional Rajasthani mud houses
- Smaller, quieter sand dunes
- Village life away from the main tourist strip

## When to go

Overnight, if you want a desert camp experience with fewer crowds than Sam.

## Getting there

Southwest of Jaisalmer, near Sam.

~50 minutes by taxi from the fort.

## Visitor tips

Ask your camp or driver in advance about facilities — options are simpler here than at the larger Sam camps.
