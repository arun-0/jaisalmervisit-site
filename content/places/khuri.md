## What makes it worth visiting

A calmer alternative to Sam — smaller crowds, traditional mud-and-thatch houses, and dunes right at the edge of the village.

## What to look for

- Traditional Rajasthani mud houses
- Smaller, quieter sand dunes
- Village life away from the main tourist strip

## When to go

Overnight, if you want a desert camp experience with fewer crowds than Sam.

## Getting there

Southwest of Jaisalmer. Khuri and Sam are separate destinations; confirm the exact camp location before arranging a transfer.

~50 minutes by taxi from the fort.

## Khuri or Sam?

Choose the particular stay and outing rather than relying on the village name alone. [Sam](/places/sam-sand-dunes) is the main safari hub in this guide; Khuri offers a village-based alternative. Ask each camp about nearby music, group activities and the dune route if a quieter visit matters to you.

## Plan your safari

[Explore camel safari details and local options](/#things-to-do?activity=camel-safari). Confirm that the operator actually serves Khuri, where the ride starts, how long you ride and whether transport from Jaisalmer is included. Do not assume that a Sam listing includes a Khuri transfer.

## Before booking a camp

- Ask for the exact location and a written total for your dates and group.
- Confirm the tent or room, bathroom arrangement, bedding and meals.
- Check which rides or performances are included and which cost extra.
- Agree pickup, return transport, cancellation terms and the booking contact.

## Visitor tips

Ask your camp or driver in advance about facilities — facilities vary by property. The [Rajasthan Tourism guide](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/jaisalmer.html) lists camel safaris at Khuri and Sam. Checked 21 November 2026; confirm current services directly with the provider.
