## What makes it worth visiting

People tell several stories about the families who left Kuldhara. The overnight departure is part of local legend. The stone street plan and house foundations are what you can see on the ground today.

## What to look for

- Rows of abandoned stone houses
- A small on-site museum and temple
- Open desert views from the village edge

## When to go

Late afternoon, before heading toward Sam Dunes for sunset.

## Getting there

Southwest of Jaisalmer city.

~25–30 minutes by taxi from the fort.

## Visitor tips

Entry ticket required.

There is an entry ticket. It pairs naturally with a Sam Dunes sunset trip on the same route.
