## What makes it worth visiting

Built to store water for a desert city, the lake is now Jaisalmer's most relaxed public space — ghats, temples, and migratory birds in winter.

## What to look for

- The carved entrance gateway, Tilon Ki Pol
- Small temples and chhatris along the banks
- Migratory birds in the cooler months

## When to go

Sunset, for boating and the golden light on the water.

## Getting there

Southeast edge of the old city.

~15 minute walk from Jaisalmer Fort.

## Visitor tips

Boat rides are available on the lake; agree the price and duration before boarding.
