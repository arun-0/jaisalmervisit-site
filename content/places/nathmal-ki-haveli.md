## What makes it worth visiting

Local lore holds that two brothers carved each half of the facade independently — the result is a building that is almost, but not quite, symmetrical.

## What to look for

- The two subtly different halves of the main facade
- Detailed stone elephants flanking the entrance
- Fine wall paintings in parts of the interior

## When to go

Morning, paired with Patwon and Salim Singh havelis.

## Getting there

Old city, near Patwon Ki Haveli.

~10 minute walk from Jaisalmer Fort.

## Visitor tips

Look closely at the two halves of the facade to spot the asymmetry — it's easy to miss at a glance.
