## What makes it worth visiting

Considered the grandest haveli complex in Jaisalmer — the carved sandstone facades and jharokha balconies show off the city's stone-carving tradition at its peak.

## What to look for

- Intricately latticed stone windows (jharokhas)
- Mirror and mural work inside select havelis
- The narrow lane view looking up at all five facades together

## When to go

Mid-morning, when the lane is quieter and light hits the carvings well.

## Getting there

Old city, a short walk from the fort.

~10 minute walk from Jaisalmer Fort.

## Visitor tips

Ticketed interior visit for part of the complex; the exterior is free to see from the lane.

Only part of the complex is open for a ticketed interior visit; the exterior can be appreciated for free from the lane.
