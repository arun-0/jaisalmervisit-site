## What makes it worth visiting

Built for a former prime minister of the state, its top floor flares outward and is often compared to a ship's stern or peacock — one of the more unusual silhouettes among Jaisalmer's havelis.

## What to look for

- The overhanging blue-tinged upper balcony
- Carved brackets shaped like peacocks
- Views back toward the fort from the rooftop

## When to go

Late afternoon light works well on the carved facade.

## Getting there

Below the fort, old city.

~12 minute walk from Jaisalmer Fort.

## Visitor tips

Smaller than Patwon Ki Haveli — pair it with the other two havelis in one loop rather than visiting alone.
