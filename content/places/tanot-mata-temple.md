## What makes it worth visiting

Pilgrims come to Tanot Mata Temple to worship. The temple also preserves local accounts of its survival during the 1965 war. Treat the shrine and the exhibits as separate parts of the visit.

## What to look for

- Unexploded shells from the 1965 war, displayed in the temple museum
- The Vijay Stambh, a victory pillar built after the 1971 war
- Daily aarti performed by BSF personnel
- Wind farms and open dunes along the long drive out

## When to go

An early start from Jaisalmer, reaching by late morning; November to January is the most comfortable season.

## Getting there

Tanot, near the India–Pakistan border in Jaisalmer district.

~120 km from the city, about 2–2.5 hours by taxi.

## Visitor tips

Plan it as a full-day trip and carry water, snacks and ID, since facilities along the route are limited. It is a border area, so access can be restricted at times of tension; check before setting out. Pair it with Longewala on the same drive.
