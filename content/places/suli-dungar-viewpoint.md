## What makes it worth visiting

Often simply called "Sunset Point" by locals, this quiet platform above the city gives one of the best panoramas of Jaisalmer Fort — especially as the sandstone glows at sunset and the fort lights come on after dark.

## What to look for

- A full panorama of Jaisalmer Fort and the old city
- Sunset colours spreading across the sandstone rooftops
- The fort lit up after dusk
- A small hilltop temple

## When to go

Arrive 30–40 minutes before sunset and stay until the fort lights come on.

## Getting there

West side of the city, on the small hill above the Artist Colony, off Maka Pol.

A short auto ride or uphill walk from the old city.

## Visitor tips

The last stretch is a short climb on uneven ground, so wear shoes with grip and carry a phone torch for the walk down after dark. There is a small temple on top, so be respectful of worshippers.
