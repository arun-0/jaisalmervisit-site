## What makes it worth visiting

The museum’s galleries tell the story of military service in the Thar, including the Battle of Longewala. Allow time for the indoor displays as well as the exhibits outside.

## What to look for

- Tanks, guns and military vehicles, including equipment captured in 1965 and 1971
- The Hunter aircraft that attacked enemy tank columns at Longewala
- Longewala Hall and Indian Army Hall, with models, maps and photographs
- The Honour Wall with names of Param Vir Chakra and Maha Vir Chakra awardees

## When to go

Morning or late afternoon; stay into the evening if the light and sound show is running.

## Getting there

Military Station, on the Jaisalmer–Jodhpur highway.

~10 km from the city, about 20 minutes by taxi.

## Visitor tips

Entry charges apply and can change — confirm locally.

Allow 1–2 hours, including the short film in the Audio-Visual Room. Entry charges and show timings can change, so confirm locally before you go. It sits on the Jodhpur highway, so it fits easily into your arrival or departure day.
