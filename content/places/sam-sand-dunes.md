## What makes it worth visiting

The most accessible large dune field near the city, and the main hub for camel and jeep safaris, sunset viewing, and desert camps.

## Choose your desert visit

A sunset visit, a safari and an overnight camp are different products. Start with the experience you want, then compare what each operator includes.

- Sunset visit: confirm the dune location, pickup point and return time.
- Camel safari: ask for the actual riding time, rather than the total outing duration.
- Jeep safari: check whether the price covers a desert drive or only a transfer to the dunes.
- Overnight camp: confirm the room or tent, bathroom, meals and any activities separately.

[See camel safari details and local options](/#things-to-do?activity=camel-safari) or [explore jeep safari options](/#things-to-do?activity=jeep-safari). Listings are a starting point for enquiries; confirm availability and terms directly with the provider.

## What to look for

- Rolling sand dunes stretching to the horizon
- Sunset over the dune field
- Camel and jeep safari operators along the access road

## When to go

Late afternoon into sunset — arrive with enough time before dusk.

## Getting there

~40 km west of Jaisalmer city.

~45–60 minutes by taxi from the fort.

## Visitor tips

Sam is the main safari and camp hub in this guide. If you prefer a village-based stay, compare [Khuri](/places/khuri). A quieter setting depends on the specific camp, route and date; ask what else is nearby before paying.

## Before you pay

Get a written total for your group and dates, including transfers, meals, activities and any extras. Check cancellation terms, the return journey and who will handle a change to your booking. A low headline price is difficult to compare until the inclusions match.

The [Rajasthan Tourism Jaisalmer guide](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/jaisalmer.html) confirms camel safari options at both Sam and Khuri. Checked 21 November 2026; prices and availability must be confirmed with the operator.
