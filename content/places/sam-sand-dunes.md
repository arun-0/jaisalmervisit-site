## What makes it worth visiting

The most accessible large dune field near the city, and the main hub for camel and jeep safaris, sunset viewing, and desert camps.

## What to look for

- Rolling sand dunes stretching to the horizon
- Sunset over the dune field
- Camel and jeep safari operators along the access road

## When to go

Late afternoon into sunset — arrive with enough time before dusk.

## Getting there

~40 km west of Jaisalmer city.

~45–60 minutes by taxi from the fort.

## Visitor tips

This is the busiest dune site near the city. For a quieter dune experience, Khuri is a calmer alternative.
