## What makes it worth visiting

Families live inside Jaisalmer Fort, alongside temples, shops and guesthouses. Enter on foot through the gates to see how the old defensive route becomes a neighbourhood street.

## What to look for

- The four massive gates along the entry ramp
- Jain temples inside the fort walls, carved from the same sandstone
- Raj Mahal, the former royal palace
- Narrow inner lanes lined with carved havelis and craft shops

## When to go

Early morning or just before sunset, to avoid the midday heat and catch the stone turning gold.

## Getting there

Central Jaisalmer, on Trikuta Hill.

Walkable from most hotels in the old city.

## Visitor tips

Wear shoes with grip — the ramp and inner lanes are uneven stone. Many havelis and shops inside are private homes; ask before photographing residents.
