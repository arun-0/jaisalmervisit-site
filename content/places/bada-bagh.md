## What makes it worth visiting

Each domed chhatri commemorates a former ruler, making the site a compact, visual timeline of the Bhati dynasty — especially striking against a sunset sky.

## What to look for

- Rows of carved cenotaphs on the hillside
- Wind turbines and open desert views beyond the site
- A small dam and garden below the chhatris

## When to go

Sunset — the site is oriented for it and gets busy right before dusk.

## Getting there

A few kilometres north of the city.

~15–20 minutes by taxi or auto from the fort.

## Visitor tips

Combine with Kuldhara and Sam Dunes if you're hiring a taxi for the afternoon — they sit roughly along the same route.
